# Seven-Agent Formal Acceptance

Machine gate: `wpe.model-off-aggregate-acceptance/1.0` in `Services/Agent/ModelOffAggregateAcceptanceV1.cs`.

Each aggregate Agent remains `partial` until its own four required evidence classes pass together. Component acceptance, a passing unit test, a live process, or a public price response cannot promote an aggregate. All seven aggregate Agents are formally accepted only within their exact candidate-bound scopes recorded in the maturity authority.

Every evidence record must bind one requirement and Agent to the required `Local`, `Target`, or `Testnet` environment, a UTC observation and bounded expiry, a passing result, canonical artifact bytes whose recomputed lowercase SHA-256 matches the declared identity, and a bounded provider identity for live evidence. Missing, duplicate, cross-Agent, unexpected, stale, overlong, failed, malformed, hash-mismatched, or wrong-environment evidence fails closed. Mainnet is not an accepted evidence environment.

The current gates cover:

- Market: canonical contract, adversarial failure behavior, live provider read, sustained freshness.
- Research: canonical contract, point-in-time replay, live source availability, model-off target cycle.
- Strategy: lifecycle contract, anti-overfit validation, Testnet shadow observation, no Risk Gate bypass.
- Risk: deterministic gate, adversarial bypass rejection, live preauthorization, time-of-use revalidation.
- Execution: mutation routing, idempotency, live Testnet order lifecycle, exchange correlation.
- Recovery: restart reconciliation, unknown quarantine, live timeout reconciliation, no-resubmit proof.
- Audit: append-only enforcement, seven-role correlation, live-cycle completeness, restart durability.

The gate produces a deterministic evidence-set hash but does not edit the maturity authority. Promotion requires an explicit reviewed update to `model-off-capability-maturity.json` after the gate accepts the corresponding aggregate.

Market live evidence is produced only by `--market-aggregate-acceptance`. The runner uses the official Binance Futures Testnet endpoint with execution disabled and empty credentials. It emits one immediate read artifact and four five-minute samples spanning at least 15 minutes. Every artifact binds the executing assembly version and SHA-256, is atomically persisted, then independently reread, strictly parsed, canonicalized and hash-verified. Every sample must contain canonical Market provenance, at least 31 confirmed candles, quality score 65 or higher, a source no older than 20 minutes, and the sustained artifact must contain at least two distinct canonical market states. The runner exposes no account, order, placement, cancellation, or mutation method.

Market local evidence is produced by two independent focused TRX runs and packaged with `--market-local-acceptance`. The structural TRX parser requires the exact allowlisted test identities, matching definitions and results, all-pass counters, and no missing, duplicate, failed, or extra tests. Each canonical artifact binds its requirement ID, candidate version and DLL SHA-256, test DLL SHA-256, raw TRX SHA-256, UTC completion time, and exact sorted test set. The command is read-only and cannot promote maturity by itself.

Research local evidence uses the same structural verifier through `--research-local-acceptance`, but has separate exact allowlists and independent TRX files. `research.canonical-contract` covers canonical News, Macro, Technical, Fundamental and Backtest admission. `research.point-in-time-replay` covers symbol/range isolation, publication-time ordering, preserved validation timestamps and source-specific expiry. These local artifacts do not satisfy either Target requirement and cannot promote Research without live-source availability and a complete model-off target cycle from the same candidate.

Research Target evidence is produced by `--research-aggregate-acceptance`. It reads no credentials and exposes no mutation path. The runner requires canonical Binance Futures Testnet BTCUSDT market and venue-instrument facts, both allowlisted BLS series, at least one valid allowlisted HTTPS news source, and a newly computed five-capability model-off cycle. Technical provenance uses only fully closed one-minute candles for current source time while retaining deterministic confirmed multi-timeframe signals. Official BLS `value:"-"` rows are skipped only when a bounded official footnote explicitly states `Data unavailable`; unfootnoted or arbitrary nonnumeric values remain invalid. `--research-acceptance-assess` requires both local and both Target artifacts to bind the executing candidate before the common aggregate gate can accept Research.

Strategy local evidence is produced by three independent focused TRX runs and packaged with `--strategy-local-acceptance`. Exact allowlists bind lifecycle isolation and lineage, chronological anti-overfit gates, and the mandatory pre-authorization no-bypass boundary to one candidate DLL and test assembly. `--strategy-aggregate-acceptance` adds the separate Testnet requirement using 24 consecutive, distinct, fully closed one-minute BTCUSDT candles from the execution-disabled Binance Futures Testnet adapter. Every sample binds source time, deterministic signal, price and canonical Market evidence; the final strategy must remain Shadow and the artifact declares zero mutation. `--strategy-acceptance-assess` accepts only when all four artifacts bind the executing candidate.

Risk local evidence is produced by two independent focused TRX runs and packaged with `--risk-local-acceptance`. Exact allowlists bind deterministic rule/intent hashes, stale/incomplete/account limit rejection, Mainnet rejection, the shared authorization gateway, replay rejection, and zero-mutation behavior under bypass attempts. `--risk-aggregate-acceptance` loads only the encrypted active Testnet slot, creates an execution-disabled connection copy, and hashes authoritative permission, account, position, market and rule facts without persisting their values or identity. It emits a bounded risk-increasing preauthorization plus a persisted short-lived receipt, then re-reads Testnet facts and proves current authority allowed, six-second-stale authority rejected, and conflicting policy rejected. `--risk-acceptance-assess` accepts only when both Local and both Testnet artifacts bind the executing candidate.

Execution local evidence is produced by two independent exact-allowlist TRX runs and packaged with `--execution-local-acceptance`. Mutation-route evidence binds the shared authorization gateway and sole `ReliableOrderExecutor` boundary; idempotency evidence binds review, emergency, smoke and client-order replay rejection. `--execution-aggregate-acceptance` requires an enabled official Binance Futures Testnet profile, encrypted in-process credentials, authoritative read/trade permission, positive account authority, an initially flat BTCUSDT state and a live tradable capability. It routes one rule-minimal isolated opening through `TradingExecutionGateway`, requires exact `FILLED` exchange correlation and both protection orders, then routes a reduce-only close through the same gateway and fails unless the exchange confirms the close with no residual position or protection order. Canonical artifacts retain only hashes and bounded truth flags, not credentials, account values or order identifiers. `--execution-acceptance-assess` accepts only when both Local and both Testnet artifacts bind the executing candidate.

Recovery local evidence is produced by two independent exact-allowlist TRX runs and packaged with `--recovery-local-acceptance`. Restart evidence binds persisted SQLite recovery, ambiguous execution reconciliation, concurrent restart claiming and terminal non-replay. Unknown-quarantine evidence binds zero replacement submissions, unknown-state preservation and a production source guard that the reconciliation path cannot call execution. `--recovery-aggregate-acceptance` submits one rule-minimal, non-marketable BTCUSDT limit order through the Review authorization gateway, waits for the production timeout, refreshes live provider capability before cancellation, and requires a zero-fill terminal exchange order. It then reopens the persisted database and performs two read-only reconciliations; both must converge on the same terminal order while no position or open order remains. Failed attempts and cleanup defects remain preserved as rejected artifacts. `--recovery-acceptance-assess` accepts only when both Local and both Testnet artifacts bind the executing candidate.

Audit local evidence is produced by two independent exact-allowlist TRX runs and packaged with `--audit-local-acceptance`. Append-only evidence binds database mutation rejection and transaction rollback on handoff identity conflict. Correlation evidence binds the ordered seven-output/six-handoff production chain, zero mutation and the mandatory persisted risk-increase gate. `--audit-aggregate-acceptance` reads current public BTCUSDT evidence from the official Binance Futures Testnet without credentials or mutation authority, persists an audit-only canonical seven-role chain through the production orchestrator, reopens SQLite, replays the same cycle idempotently and requires every output and handoff hash to remain unchanged. This certifies Audit persistence and correlation only; it does not certify the audit-input placeholders as upstream business decisions, trading execution or profitability. `--audit-acceptance-assess` accepts only when both Local and both Testnet artifacts bind the executing candidate.
